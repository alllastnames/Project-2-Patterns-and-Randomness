This code generates a white diamond background with a black outline. On top of the background are three separate columns of squares that vary in number and opacity. The circle in front of the squares change the amount of squares and the opacity of them when they bounce between the top and bottom of the screen. The first column has yellow squares with a pink ball, the second column has pink squares with a blue ball and the final column has blue squares with a yellow ball. The opacity of each ball is set to one value but as they pass over the squares with varying opacity the color of the ball shifts. The x value of each column of squares is set while the y value varies between multiples of 64. The minimum and maximum number of squares that can appear in a column are 1 and 4 respectively.
